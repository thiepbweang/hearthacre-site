Privacy and consent

Version 2, 2026-10-01. Hearth Acre Investments is the brand of Thiep Weang, a real estate investor buying in Texas and Tennessee. Updated 2026-10-29 to add Tennessee; the consent wording is unchanged.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: the property, your timeline, your name, phone and email, and your consent choices, plus the time you sent it.

How we use it

  • To talk with you about your property and make an offer, or to send you deals that fit your buy box.
  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • The forms are hosted by our website host (Netlify), which stores the submissions for us.

Calls and texts

We call or text only people who asked us to, or whose numbers we have checked against the National Do Not Call Registry, and only between 9 AM and 8 PM your local time (never before noon on Sundays). Calls are placed by a person. If you ask us to stop, we stop, and your number goes on our internal do-not-call list.

Automated texts and recorded or AI voice

We use them only if you checked the optional box that says so. That consent is never required to get an offer or join the list.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any text, tell us on a call, or email us. We honor opt-outs within 10 business days, usually the same day.

Assignment disclosure

We are not real estate agents or brokers. When we sign a purchase contract, we may assign it to another buyer for a fee. We tell every seller this in writing before they sign, as Texas law requires (Texas Occupations Code §1101.0045), and we market only our contract, never your property. In Tennessee, the written disclosures required by Tenn. Code 66-4-401 to 403 are included in every contract.
