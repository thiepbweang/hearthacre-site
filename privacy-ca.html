Privacy

Version 1, 2026-10-29. Hearth Acre Investments is the brand of Thiep Weang, a real estate investor buying houses in Alberta and Ontario.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email, phone, the property address, the province, any note you add, and the time you sent it.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your property and make an offer, or to send you deals that fit your buy box.
  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • The forms are hosted by our website host (Netlify), which stores the submissions for us. Its servers may be outside Canada.

Contact

When you submit a form you ask Hearth Acre Investments to contact you about your property. If you ask us to stop, we stop.

How to opt out or ask about your information

Tell us on a call or email hearthacrehomes@gmail.com to stop contact, or to ask what we hold about you or have it deleted.

Assignment

We buy as a principal. Where we may assign our purchase agreement, we say so in writing in the agreement, and we market only our agreement, never your property.
