Selling direct vs. listing with an agent

Listing can bring a higher price. A direct sale trades some of that for speed and certainty. Here is the honest comparison.

Showings and open housesOne walkthroughMany, on buyers' schedules
Commissions$0 to youOften 5 to 6 percent
Closing costsDiscussed up front in the offerUsually paid by the seller

We buy as a principal and may assign our purchase contract to another buyer, as disclosed in writing. We are not real estate agents and we do not list properties.

Questions

Do I pay any fees?

No commissions and no listing fees. Who pays closing costs is written into the offer before you sign anything.

Who handles the money and paperwork?

A local Texas title company. They check ownership, hold the earnest money and pay everyone at closing.

Do you buy as-is?

Yes. No repairs, no cleaning, no showings.

Will you buy it yourselves?

We sign the purchase contract, and we may assign that contract to another buyer, such as a builder, for a fee. We tell you this up front and in writing (Texas Occupations Code §1101.0045), and your price stays the price in the contract.

How fast can you close?

It depends on the title search and the property. You pick the date that works for you.

What happens to my information?

We use it only to talk with you about your property or the deal list. We never sell it. See the privacy page.
